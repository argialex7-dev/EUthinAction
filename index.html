02 / Editorial

Welcome to Đakovo

A note from the organisers

Welcome to the first edition of The EUth Times. This small newspaper tells the story of nine days in Đakovo, where young people from different countries met to learn from each other, question what they know and turn ideas into action.

We explored three big questions: How do we live well in a digital world? What does a greener Europe really ask of us? And what makes rural places worth fighting for?

Young people are not only the future of Europe. They are already part of its present.

Thank you to every participant, group leader, facilitator and volunteer. Turn the page and enjoy.

Learning by doing – from the first ice-breaker to the final idea

05 / Theme 1 · Digital life

Think before you scroll

Social media, footprints and digital citizenship

We spend a large part of our lives online. The digital sessions asked a simple question: who is in control, us or the screen?

Risks, benefits, wellbeing

Groups mapped connection, creativity and information against comparison, fake news and screen fatigue. Balance is a skill.

Footprint & citizenship

Every post leaves a trace. Digital citizens respect others, protect privacy, check sources and use their voice for good.

Being connected is not the same as being close.PARTICIPANT REFLECTION

Tools for local change

How can apps and social media help solve a problem in your own town or village? Teams turned tools into action.

06 / Theme 2 · Rural realities

“Sell us your village!”

Young people reimagine life outside the city

Rural areas are often seen as places to leave. The exchange flipped that story: what would make young people want to stay – or come back?

ChallengesFewer services, long distances, youth migration
StrengthsNature, tradition, tight-knit communities
OpportunitiesRemote work, local business, green tourism

Reimagining rural spaces

Teams redesigned a village hall, a square or an old school into places that bring young people together – then pitched their villages to everyone.

Every village has a story. We just have to tell it well.

07 / Theme 3 · Green Europe

Green or greenwashed?

Whose responsibility is it to protect our planet?

Everyone talks about being “green” – but how can we tell what is real? Participants learned to ask: is the claim specific, proven and honest – or just a nice picture of a leaf?

Green initiatives in Europe

Groups presented inspiring projects, from community gardens to energy cooperatives, and discussed what could work at home.

One person’s trash is another person’s treasure.

European Green Future Museum

Teams built a mini-museum of the future with exhibits, stories and solutions for a sustainable Europe.
